# Community code of conduct

YouthOpp welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat contributors, opportunity seekers and publishers with respect. Critique work with evidence and explain how it can improve.

## Expected behaviour

Be considerate, credit other people's work, welcome questions and respect privacy. Use accessible language and help new contributors learn. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending a source or opportunity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualised conduct, personal attacks, publication of private information and deliberate misrepresentation are unacceptable. Spam, fraudulent opportunities and manipulation of contribution records are also prohibited.

## Reporting and response

Report public conduct problems to repository maintainers through a GitHub issue when doing so does not expose private information. For sensitive reports, contact a maintainer using a contact method they have explicitly published on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in public. A dedicated private reporting channel has not yet been established.

Maintainers may request changes, remove content, restrict participation or block accounts, depending on the severity and recurrence of behaviour. They should explain decisions where doing so is appropriate and protects affected people. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

This policy applies to YouthOpp repositories and project-controlled community spaces. It is not a promise that maintainers can moderate unrelated third-party services.
